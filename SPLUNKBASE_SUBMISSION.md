## Positioning

MISPFuse is an outbound MISP publishing app. It maps Splunk fields through
editable templates, validates mappings against the destination MISP taxonomy,
tests account settings before saving, supports multiple MISP accounts, and
provides both an SPL command and an alert action. It does not collect MISP data
into Splunk.

Suggested categories: Threat Intelligence and Security, Fraud & Compliance.

Suggested tags: `misp`, `threat intelligence`, `ioc`, `indicator sharing`,
`alert action`, `custom search command`.
